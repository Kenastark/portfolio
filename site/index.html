Provenance

An AI trust layer for environmental sensor networks.

By every conventional measure, Debrecen’s Green Sentinel network looked healthy. Provenance audited 149,683 readings from 16 stations and found a large share quietly wrong. Then it asks the harder question: did a pollution event travel the way real pollutant transport would? A wind-conditioned graph over the stations separates a plume from a broken sensor, and every call ships with reason codes, SHAP attributions and a calibrated confidence interval. Nothing reaches the public without a human sign-off.

  • Audit16 statistical detectors, deterministic and test-gated (117 tests, 96% coverage at phase 1)
  • ModelHST-GAT, a heterogeneous spatio-temporal graph attention network with 3,299 parameters, plus split-conformal intervals
  • StackPython, FastAPI, PostGIS, LightGBM, PyTorch Geometric, React + TypeScript, MapLibre

CBHMIS anomaly detection

Which mortality figures are real, and which are reporting errors?

As part of CHAI’s data quality assessments, I built unsupervised anomaly detection over Nigeria’s community-based health management information system (CBHMIS). For every ward and LGA in Kaduna and Gombe states, the pipeline turns raw counts into maternal deaths per 100,000 live births and neonatal deaths per 1,000, then flags statistically unusual areas with Isolation Forest, cross-checked against Local Outlier Factor and One-Class SVM. The flags surfaced possible hotspots alongside data problems, such as ward names spelled several ways and deaths reported against zero live births, and flagged areas were recommended for field verification before anyone acted on the numbers.

  • MethodsIsolation Forest, Local Outlier Factor and One-Class SVM consensus; K-Means clustering of wards (silhouette-selected k)
  • StackPython, pandas, scikit-learn, Streamlit, Plotly, DHIS2, Power BI

A faculty-verified internship and entry-level hiring platform, built against ghost jobs. Companies are approved before they can post, students verify with their university email, and posting means committing to hire. When a hire happens, the job and candidate IDs are matched on a public ledger and the posting comes down: proof the job was real.

Outbreak GNN

Spatiotemporal graph neural networks for early outbreak detection and risk prediction.

What if disease surveillance could see an outbreak coming the way a weather model sees a front? My thesis builds dynamic proximity graphs over U.S. geographic zones, with edges for mobility and adjacency, and trains graph neural networks to predict node-level infection risk. An anomaly-detection module flags unusual signals, ablations test what weather, mobility and population density really contribute, and a decision-support dashboard puts the result in front of the people who have to act.

  • MethodsGCN, GAT and temporal GNN variants; Isolation Forest and autoencoder anomaly detection
  • DataGoogle COVID-19 Open Data, United States
  • GroundingAnnotated bibliography of 23 papers across five research themes
